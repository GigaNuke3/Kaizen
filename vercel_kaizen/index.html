Everything you need to keep going

No accounts, no subscriptions. Just you, your goals and a couple of pixel friends cheering you on.

Goals that fit your week

Daily, weekdays, weekends, 3× a week or custom days, each with its own time window, colour and label.

CheckMarked: earn every tick

Every check needs a journal entry for that goal. Checks are permanent, and missed days close at midnight. No cheating yourself.

Journal with moods

Write a reflection, pick how you feel, and look back on how your mood moves over time. Export it any time.

Kai & Zun, your companions

A 12-month activity grid, a radar chart of your goal labels, live stats and a weekly review.

Badges & ranks

Hit milestones, unlock badges and level up your companion's rank as your streaks pile up.
